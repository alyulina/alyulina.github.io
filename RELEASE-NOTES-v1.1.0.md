**Pinned header**: on laptops and tablets, the name and menu stay at the top while scrolling and never move between pages, with plain white under the menu and a soft fade. Phones keep the normal header. On the Publications page, the equal-contribution note stays at the bottom of the window while the list scrolls.

**New accent colour and an optional cursor**: the pink is now clay. The bacteria cursor is off by default and can be switched on with the bacterium at the end of the menu (on computers with a mouse or trackpad); the choice is remembered.

**Proper phone layout**: a real mobile layout with readable text, easier-to-tap navigation and no sideways scrolling. Upgraded to MathJax 4, so long equations break across lines on small screens.

**Publications**: papers can show their abstract with a click, and the list also works without JavaScript.

**Updates on the About page**: a short, dated list of recent and upcoming talks and papers.

**No outside servers**: MathJax and its font are served from this site, so visitors' browsers don't contact third-party servers.

**Easier to fork**: personal details are set in one place, `_config.yml`, and the README has an updated checklist.
